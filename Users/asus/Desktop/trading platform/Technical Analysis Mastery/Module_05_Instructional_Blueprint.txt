SECTION 1: MODULE BLUEPRINT
================================================================================

1. Module Title:
   Module 5: Trend Lines & Channels (خطوط الاتجاه والقنوات السعرية)

2. Module Description:
   This module provides a rigorous, CMT-aligned breakdown of linear trendlines, parallel trend channels, trend angles/slopes, Schabacker & Edwards/Magee sustainability rules, the Fan Principle (3-line trend decay), and trendline breakout validation using time and price filters.

3. Module Goal:
   To equip students with mathematically sound, objective drawing rules for constructing valid trendlines, equidistant parallel channels, and applying classical breakout filter rules without slicing price candles or drawing subjective lines.

4. Learning Outcomes:
   Upon completing this module, students will be able to:
   - Construct valid Uptrend and Downtrend lines using CMT 3-touch validation rules.
   - Evaluate trendline slopes and sustainability (45-degree angle benchmarks vs. parabolic curves).
   - Construct Parallel Channels and utilize median line (mid-line) reaction dynamics.
   - Apply Schabacker's Fan Principle (3-Line Rule) to spot progressive trend decay.
   - Validate trendline breaks using price filters (3% rule) and time filters (2-day rule).

5. Skills Students Will Acquire:
   - Geometric Trendline Drawing (2-Touch Tentative, 3-Touch Confirmed)
   - Parallel Trend Channel Construction
   - Trend Sustainability & Slope Analysis
   - Fan Principle (3-Line) Trend Exhaustion Spotting
   - Time & Price Breakout Filter Application

6. Prerequisites:
   - Completion of Modules 1 through 4.

7. Estimated Completion Time:
   - Total Duration: 10.0 Hours
   - Video Content: 2.5 Hours (150 minutes)
   - Reading & Multimedia: 2.5 Hours
   - Interactive Practice & TradingView Drills: 3.5 Hours
   - Quizzes & Assessments: 1.5 Hours

8. Difficulty Level:
   - Intermediate (Level 200 / University Core Course Level)

9. Recommended Learning Order:
   - Step 1: Lesson 5.1 — Geometry & Rules of Valid Trend Line Construction
   - Step 2: Lesson 5.2 — Trend Angles, Slopes, and Sustainability
   - Step 3: Lesson 5.3 — Parallel Channels: Equidistant Construction & Mid-Line Dynamics

   - Step 5: Lesson 5.5 — Trendline Breaks, Filter Rules, and Re-tests
   - Step 6: Module 5 Practical Project & Comprehensive Examination

SECTION 2: LESSON BLUEPRINTS

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LESSON 5.1: GEOMETRY & RULES OF VALID TREND LINE CONSTRUCTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lesson Number: Lesson 5.1
- Lesson Title: Geometry & Rules of Valid Trend Line Construction
- Lesson Goal: Master the mathematical and geometric rules for drawing valid trendlines without slicing candle bodies.
- Learning Objectives:
  1. Define a Tentative Trendline (2 touches) vs. Confirmed Trendline (3 touches).
  2. Draw Uptrend lines connecting Higher Lows and Downtrend lines connecting Lower Highs.
  3. Filter out invalid trendlines that slice through price candles.
- Prerequisite Knowledge: Modules 1-4.
- Estimated Study Time: 110 Minutes (Video: 30m, Reading: 40m, Practice: 40m)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LESSON 5.2: TREND ANGLES, SLOPES, AND SUSTAINABILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lesson Number: Lesson 5.2
- Lesson Title: Trend Angles, Slopes, and Sustainability
- Lesson Goal: Analyze trendline slopes and assess trend sustainability using Schabacker & Edwards/Magee angle rules.
- Learning Objectives:
  1. Identify optimal 45-degree sustainable trend slopes.
  2. Spot unsustainable parabolic curves (> 60 degrees) leading to blow-offs.
  3. Spot weak trends (< 20 degrees) prone to consolidation degradation.
- Prerequisite Knowledge: Lesson 5.1.
- Estimated Study Time: 110 Minutes (Video: 30m, Reading: 40m, Practice: 40m)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LESSON 5.3: PARALLEL CHANNELS: EQUIDISTANT CONSTRUCTION & MID-LINE DYNAMICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lesson Number: Lesson 5.3
- Lesson Title: Parallel Channels: Equidistant Construction & Mid-Line Dynamics
- Lesson Goal: Construct parallel trend channels and utilize the median line (mid-line) for action-reaction analysis.
- Learning Objectives:
  1. Construct Equidistant Bullish, Bearish, and Sideways Parallel Channels.
  2. Analyze mid-line (50% median) support/resistance reactions.
  3. Project measured move targets upon channel breakouts.
- Prerequisite Knowledge: Lesson 5.2.
- Estimated Study Time: 120 Minutes (Video: 30m, Reading: 45m, Practice: 45m)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LESSON 5.4: THE FAN PRINCIPLE (3-LINE TREND DECAY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lesson Number: Lesson 5.4
- Lesson Title: The Fan Principle (3-Line Trend Decay)
- Lesson Goal: Apply Schabacker's Fan Principle (3 progressive trendlines) to identify complete structural trend reversals.
- Learning Objectives:
  1. Construct 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Fan Lines as trend slope flattens.
  2. Identify the break of the 3rd Fan Line as a definitive trend reversal signal.
  3. Integrate Volume confirmation across Fan Line breakdowns.
- Prerequisite Knowledge: Lessons 5.1 - 5.3.
- Estimated Study Time: 120 Minutes (Video: 30m, Reading: 45m, Practice: 45m)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LESSON 5.5: TRENDLINE BREAKS, FILTER RULES, AND RE-TESTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lesson Number: Lesson 5.5
- Lesson Title: Trendline Breaks, Filter Rules (Time/Price Filters), and Re-tests
- Lesson Goal: Validate trendline breakouts using the 3% price filter and 2-bar time filter.
- Learning Objectives:
  1. Apply the 3% Price Breakout Filter (price closes 3% beyond trendline).
  2. Apply the 2-Bar Time Breakout Filter (price remains beyond trendline for 2 full bars).
  3. Trade trendline retests with high probability.
- Prerequisite Knowledge: Lessons 5.1 - 5.4.
- Estimated Study Time: 130 Minutes (Video: 30m, Reading: 50m, Practice: 50m)
