SECTION 1: MODULE BLUEPRINT
================================================================================

1. Module Title:
   Module 3: Price Structure (هيكل السعر)

2. Module Description:
   This module establishes structural mastery in reading price action, defining swing points (fractal highs/lows), mapping bullish peak-and-valley progressions (Higher Highs & Higher Lows), tracking bearish degradation (Lower Highs & Lower Lows), validating structural breaks (classical Break of Structure), and identifying range-bound or structureless price environments.

3. Module Goal:
   To equip students with a systematic, objective methodology for mapping market structure across any chart, eliminating subjectivity in identifying swing points, trend continuations, and structural trend transitions.

4. Learning Outcomes:
   Upon completing this module, students will be able to:
   - Systematically define and map 3-bar and 5-bar fractal swing highs and swing lows.
   - Construct Bullish Market Structure progressions (HH + HL) with 100% visual precision.
   - Construct Bearish Market Structure progressions (LH + LL) with 100% visual precision.
   - Validate classical structural breaks upon price closing beyond key swing pivots.
   - Identify ranging and structureless price environments and avoid low-probability breakout traps.

5. Skills Students Will Acquire:
   - Objective Swing Point Identification (3-Bar & 5-Bar Rule)
   - Bullish & Bearish Market Structure Mapping
   - Structural Break Confirmation (Classical Shift in Market Structure)
   - Consolidation/Range Boundary Definition
   - Path-Tool Chart Mapping on TradingView

6. Prerequisites:
   - Completion of Module 1 (Introduction to Financial Markets) and Module 2 (Dow Theory).

7. Estimated Completion Time:
   - Total Duration: 10.0 Hours
   - Video Content: 2.5 Hours (150 minutes)
   - Reading & Multimedia: 2.5 Hours
   - Interactive Practice & TradingView Drills: 3.5 Hours
   - Quizzes & Assessments: 1.5 Hours

8. Difficulty Level:
   - Intermediate (Level 200 / University Core Course Level)

9. Recommended Learning Order:
   - Step 1: Lesson 3.1 — Anatomy of Swing Points (Fractal Highs & Lows)
   - Step 2: Lesson 3.2 — Bullish Market Structure: Peaks & Valleys Progression
   - Step 3: Lesson 3.3 — Bearish Market Structure: Degradation & Lower Pivots
   - Step 4: Lesson 3.4 — Market Structure Transitions & Structural Breaks
   - Step 5: Lesson 3.5 — Ranging & Structureless Price Environments
   - Step 6: Module 3 Practical Project & Comprehensive Examination

SECTION 2: LESSON BLUEPRINTS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LESSON 3.1: ANATOMY OF SWING POINTS (FRACTAL HIGHS & LOWS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lesson Number: Lesson 3.1
- Lesson Title: Anatomy of Swing Points (Fractal Highs & Lows)
- Lesson Goal: Systematically identify objective swing highs and swing lows using strict N-bar fractal rules.
- Learning Objectives:
  1. Define a 3-bar and 5-bar Swing High (High candle flanked by lower highs).
  2. Define a 3-bar and 5-bar Swing Low (Low candle flanked by higher lows).
  3. Filter out minor intra-bar noise from true structural swing points.
- Prerequisite Knowledge: Modules 1 & 2.
- Estimated Study Time: 110 Minutes (Video: 30m, Reading: 40m, Practice: 40m)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LESSON 3.2: BULLISH MARKET STRUCTURE: PEAKS & VALLEYS PROGRESSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lesson Number: Lesson 3.2
- Lesson Title: Bullish Market Structure: Peaks & Valleys Progression
- Lesson Goal: Map bullish price expansion characterized by Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL).
- Learning Objectives:
  1. Construct a valid Bullish Market Structure sequence.
  2. Differentiate a confirmed Higher Low from an unconfirmed pullback.
  3. Validate structural continuation upon breaking the prior swing high.
- Prerequisite Knowledge: Lesson 3.1.
- Estimated Study Time: 120 Minutes (Video: 30m, Reading: 45m, Practice: 45m)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LESSON 3.3: BEARISH MARKET STRUCTURE: DEGRADATION & LOWER PIVOTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lesson Number: Lesson 3.3
- Lesson Title: Bearish Market Structure: Degradation & Lower Pivots
- Lesson Goal: Map bearish price contraction characterized by Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL).
- Learning Objectives:
  1. Construct a valid Bearish Market Structure sequence.
  2. Identify confirmed Lower Highs vs. minor retracements.
  3. Validate bearish structural continuation upon breaking prior swing lows.
- Prerequisite Knowledge: Lesson 3.2.
- Estimated Study Time: 120 Minutes (Video: 30m, Reading: 45m, Practice: 45m)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LESSON 3.4: MARKET STRUCTURE TRANSITIONS & STRUCTURAL BREAKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lesson Number: Lesson 3.4
- Lesson Title: Market Structure Transitions & Structural Breaks
- Lesson Goal: Validate classical structural trend breaks (Shift in Market Structure) when price closes beyond key swing pivots.
- Learning Objectives:
  1. Define a valid classical Break of Structure (BOS) vs. a wick liquidity sweep.
  2. Map structural trend transitions from Bullish to Bearish and vice-versa.
  3. Identify structural invalidation points.
- Prerequisite Knowledge: Lessons 3.1 - 3.3.
- Estimated Study Time: 130 Minutes (Video: 30m, Reading: 50m, Practice: 50m)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LESSON 3.5: RANGING & STRUCTURELESS PRICE ENVIRONMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lesson Number: Lesson 3.5
- Lesson Title: Ranging & Structureless Price Environments
- Lesson Goal: Detect sideways consolidations, equal highs/lows, and structureless price noise.
- Learning Objectives:
  1. Identify Horizontal Ranges and Consolidation Zones.
  2. Recognize structureless price degradation (choppy markets).
  3. Avoid trading in low-probability non-trending environments.
- Prerequisite Knowledge: Lessons 3.1 - 3.4.
- Estimated Study Time: 120 Minutes (Video: 30m, Reading: 45m, Practice: 45m)
