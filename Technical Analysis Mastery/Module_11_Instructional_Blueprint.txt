1. Module Title:
   Module 11: Technical Indicators & Oscillators (المؤشرات والمذبذبات الفنية)

2. Module Description:
   This module provides an academically sound, CMT-aligned exploration of classical technical indicators and oscillators. Students will master Trend-Following Indicators (Moving Averages, MACD), Momentum Oscillators (RSI, Stochastic), Volatility Indicators (Bollinger Bands, ATR), Classical Divergence Theory (Regular vs. Hidden), Multi-Indicator Confluence, and Failure Swing Filtering.

3. Module Goal:
   To equip students with precise quantitative indicator evaluation skills, eliminating indicator over-fitting and treating indicators strictly as secondary validation tools for price structure.

4. Prerequisites: Completion of Modules 1-10. Total Duration: 14.0 Hours.
